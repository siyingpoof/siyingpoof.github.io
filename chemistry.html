Chemistry Course — Frequently Asked Questions

The crash course follows the MOE O Level Chemistry syllabuses for Singapore — Pure Chemistry (5073) and Combined Science Chemistry (5124). Both are covered, with separate tracks so content is appropriate for each group.

The course consists of multiple 3-hour sessions spread across the June holidays. The full schedule (dates and times) will be shared when you register — or contact us directly for details.

If you miss a session, you'll still receive all the notes and materials from that session. We'll also do our best to accommodate make-up arrangements — please contact us as early as possible.

Just bring a pen and a scientific calculator. All notes, practice papers, and summary sheets are provided and included in the course fee — no need to buy anything else.

Please contact us to discuss our refund and cancellation policy. We're happy to accommodate reasonable requests — just reach out as early as possible.
